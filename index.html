Organize your Rekordbox library. Build what plays next.

Crate reads the library you already have in Rekordbox, helps you clean it up and understand it, and builds new crates from it — filtered, ordered, and ready to bring back in. Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing uploads anywhere.

Open Crate — it's free Read the quickstart

Why this exists

As a free-time DJ, I got tired of digging through the same messy Rekordbox library every time I wanted to put together a new set. Crate is the tool I wanted for myself first — something that reads what's already in Rekordbox, helps me actually see what's in there, and builds new playlists out of it without a subscription or an account. I'm sharing it in case it's useful to you too.

Quickstart

Three steps. No install, no account.

01

Export from Rekordbox

In Rekordbox: File → Export Collection in xml format. Save it anywhere you'll remember.

02

Drop it into Crate

Open the app, drag the XML file in. Your library loads instantly — nothing leaves your browser.

03

Build and export

Filter, order, save a crate. Export as .m3u8 or a merged XML, and bring it back into Rekordbox.

What's in it

Built up feature by feature from actually using it to prep sets.

Library, organized

Sortable, searchable, with your own notes and tags layered on top of what Rekordbox gives you.

Smart crate builder

Filter by BPM, harmonic key, genre, or your own genre groups. Order by a smooth harmonic mix or a target energy shape.

Genre cleanup auto

Finds genres that are probably the same thing spelled differently, and suggests merging them.

Gigs & set sheets

Block out a set from multiple crates, track total runtime, export a clean printable sheet.

Recipes

Save a filter combo, not just a frozen list — re-run it later as your library grows.

Traktor export experimental

A best-effort NML export for Traktor users. Verify it on a small crate before trusting it fully.

🔒
Your library never leaves your computer.
Crate runs entirely in your browser. There's no server, no account, no upload. Your data is stored locally in your browser and exportable any time as a plain file you control.
